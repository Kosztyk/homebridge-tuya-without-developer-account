# Vendor artwork

`assets/homebridge-icon.png` is the official TUYA vendor logo prepared as a 100 × 100 PNG for the Homebridge plugin catalog. Its proportions and artwork are preserved.

- Official source page: https://www.tuya.com/
- Original asset: inline SVG in official website header

The logo identifies the devices supported by this independent community plugin. The plugin is not affiliated with or endorsed by the manufacturer. Vendor names, logos and artwork remain the property of their respective owners and are not included in this repository's software license.
